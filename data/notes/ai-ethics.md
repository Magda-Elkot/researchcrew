## Glossary (English / Arabic)
| Term | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| UNESCO | منظمة الأمم المتحدة للتربية والعلم والثقافة (اليونسكو) | The UN agency for education, science and culture. |
| Recommendation (UNESCO) | توصية | A standard-setting text adopted by member states. Not a binding treaty. |
| Ethical impact assessment | تقييم الأثر الأخلاقي | Checking an AI system's effects on people and society before and during use. |
| Readiness assessment methodology (RAM) | منهجية تقييم الجاهزية | UNESCO's tool to measure how ready a country is to implement the Recommendation. |
| Transparency and explainability | الشفافية وقابلية التفسير | People can understand how an AI system works and why it decided something. |
| Human oversight | الرقابة البشرية | Humans stay responsible and can intervene in AI decisions. |
| Fairness and non-discrimination | الإنصاف وعدم التمييز | AI must not treat groups of people unfairly. |
