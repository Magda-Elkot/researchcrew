## Glossary (English / Arabic)
| Term | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| WHO | منظمة الصحة العالمية | The United Nations agency for international public health. |
| EMRO / EMR | المكتب الإقليمي لشرق المتوسط / إقليم شرق المتوسط | WHO's regional office and region covering most Arab countries, plus Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan and Somalia. |
| Universal health coverage (UHC) | التغطية الصحية الشاملة | Everyone can use the health services they need without financial hardship. |
| Primary health care (PHC) | الرعاية الصحية الأولية | First-contact, community-based health services. |
| Noncommunicable diseases (NCDs) | الأمراض غير السارية | Chronic diseases such as heart disease, cancer, diabetes and chronic lung disease. |
| Health emergencies | الطوارئ الصحية | Outbreaks, conflicts and disasters that threaten health. |
| Regional Committee (RC) | اللجنة الإقليمية | The yearly meeting of the region's member states (RC72 in 2025). |
